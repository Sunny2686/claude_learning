Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect standard technical information, such as your browser type and IP address, for security and troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and improve Spendly, including to authenticate your account, display your expense history, generate spending summaries, and provide customer support. We do not use your financial data to serve you third-party advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with your account and are retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the Service; these providers only process data as needed to provide their service to us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
